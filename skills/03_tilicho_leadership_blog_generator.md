# Tilicho Leadership Blog Generator

Audience: Technical decision makers, CTOs, founders, and engineering leaders.

Required Input:
- Topic

Optional Input:
- Context
- Organizational insight
- Operational issue

Purpose:
Generate grounded operator-style leadership blogs.

Before writing:
1. Evaluate the topic
2. Improve weak framing
3. Increase operational depth
4. Strengthen systems-thinking angle
5. Generate content only after refinement
